# Sharing 100 GPU-Hours

Play one of six project teams competing for 100 shared GPU-hours.
Stage 1 is random-order first come, first served. Stage 2 is the carbon-aware
scored group selection with VCG payments from
[GihoonE/COMPSCI206-PS2](https://github.com/GihoonE/COMPSCI206-PS2).

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`. The top of `app.js` is a line-by-line port of `src/gpu_allocation.py`. Run `node app.js` to check it against the repo's outputs.
- Rival teams are simulated from the repo's Low / Medium / High tiers. Nothing is recorded.
